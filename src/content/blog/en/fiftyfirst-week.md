# Public Release of Phase 1 and Application Development

Hello, this is Haruta Watanabe.

While I usually develop mobile apps and websites, I have now completed the 51st week of my studies with a view towards a career as a software engineer and data scientist. ## Week 51 Reflection

This week, continuing from last week, I proceeded with my studies along the following two axes:

- **Public Release of Bot Detection and Rejection**
  In preparation for the Phase 2 implementation, I publicly released Phase 1 to actually measure the accuracy of bot detection and rejection on the internet. Going forward, I will improve the accuracy based on the measurement results.
- **Engineering**
  During the development of a service provided for my student club, I migrated the PWA to a separate domain. The purpose was to make it possible to switch accounts between the Web version and the PWA version. To avoid session inconsistencies caused by a session expiring on one side, I decided to provide the PWA on a separate domain.
